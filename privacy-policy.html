English

PetPaths is a free children's animal maze game published by mrsandertar. It is designed to work without accounts, advertising or an Internet connection.

Information we collect

PetPaths does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, device identifiers, location or usage data.

Information stored on the device

The app stores completed levels, custom paths and the selected language, music and sound preferences locally on the device. This information is used only to show progress, keep created paths and remember preferences. Cloud backup is disabled. The information can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children's privacy

PetPaths does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, links to online purchases, social features or user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties and changes

The app does not send information to third parties. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard web request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If the app's data practices change, this policy and the disclosures in Google Play will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to petmatch.support@gmail.com.
